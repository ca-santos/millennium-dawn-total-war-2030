Total War 2030 - Changelog

v1.0.1
- Fixed: dynamic modifiers are now actually applied (readiness 1-4 and the four flashpoint modifiers).
- Fixed: monthly pulse runs once per month again (global flag guard).
- Fixed: removed the IRN country tag (crash risk); the mod uses PER.
- Fixed: early-aggression flag checks player threat only, expires after 2 years.
- Fixed: one readiness level at a time; flags and modifiers are removed when a country stops qualifying.
- Added: wargoals for CHI/TAI, SOV/UKR, NKO/KOR, PER/ISR in Era 3; declare_war strategies only in Era 4.
- Added: bankruptcy guard on industrial and force-build AI strategies.
- Changed: readiness bonuses reduced and applied to the player too; AI building/role targets halved to avoid stacking.
- Changed: readiness recalculated on era changes and every 90 days instead of monthly.
- Changed: flashpoint news events only fire within a year of their start date.
- Changed: docs moved to docs/, descriptor tags and dependency cleaned.
